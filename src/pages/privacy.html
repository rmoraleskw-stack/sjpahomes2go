Privacy policy

This site belongs to The Morales Group, led by Raul Morales, REALTOR®, affiliated with Keller Williams Realty. This page explains what information the site collects and how it is used.

What we collect

The site collects only what you choose to send through the contact form: your name, email address, phone number if you give it, and your message. The calculators run entirely in your browser; the numbers you enter are not sent anywhere.

Analytics and cookies

With your permission, the site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it, such as which pages are viewed and how people arrive. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information like your approximate location, device and browser type. It does not tell us who you are, and it is not used for advertising.

Google Analytics does not load, and no analytics cookies are set, until you choose Accept in the cookie banner. If you choose Decline, it never loads. Your choice is saved in your browser so the banner doesn't ask again. You can change it at any time with the "Cookie choices" link at the bottom of every page; declining after accepting removes the analytics cookies from this site.

How we use it

Your message is used only to reply to your inquiry and to help with your real estate needs. It is not sold or shared with anyone for marketing.

Other websites

Links to home search, home valuation, booking (Cal.com), Zillow and YouTube take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies. A video loads from YouTube only when you press play.
